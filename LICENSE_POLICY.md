## Covered original material

Apache-2.0 applies to original project material unless a file carries a more
specific notice:

- Go source and tests;
- build, release, documentation, and repository configuration;
- public documentation and examples;
- generated reference, diagrams, terminal captures, and other generated assets;
  and
- original fixtures and templates.

Generated output does not receive a different license merely because a tool
produced it. Its generator and source inputs must remain available under their
applicable terms.

## Third-party material

Third-party material is not relicensed. Keep its copyright, license, notice,
and exact provenance with the material whenever practical. The embedded
Forecast Ledger contract and upstream fixtures retain the MIT license stored in
[`third_party/forecast-ledger/LICENSE`](third_party/forecast-ledger/LICENSE).

Resolved Go modules retain their own licenses. See
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and release SBOMs. When a
dependency, vendored file, fixture, generated input, or copied asset changes,
review its license before merging and update notices and SBOM evidence together.

Do not copy a license or copyright policy from another Chaos Condensate
repository merely because the projects are related.

## Contributions

The project does not require a Contributor License Agreement or Developer
Certificate of Origin sign-off. Material intentionally submitted for inclusion
is handled under the contribution terms of Apache-2.0. Contributors must have
the right to submit their work and must identify incorporated third-party
material and its terms.

## Commercial use

Apache-2.0 permits commercial and non-commercial use. The license does not state
that the maintainers plan to commercialize the technology, and project
documentation must not turn the maintainers' current plans into a restriction
on downstream users.
