# Forecast Ledger CLI Code of Conduct

Forecast Ledger CLI adopts and adapts the Contributor Covenant 3.0. We want a
community where people can participate safely, respectfully, and on equal
terms.

## Our commitment

We welcome people who participate in good faith and follow this policy. We
respect differences in identity, background, language, experience, viewpoint,
ability, education, nationality, religion, social position, and other personal
characteristics.

Helpful community behavior includes:

- communicating honestly and with care;
- respecting boundaries and different experiences;
- giving specific, constructive feedback about the work;
- accepting responsibility and repairing harm when possible;
- making room for questions and language differences; and
- protecting private information and unrevealed forecast material.

The following behavior is not accepted:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, stalking, or unwanted attention;
- insults, demeaning comments, identity-based attacks, or deliberate
  misgendering;
- sexualized behavior or content where it is not appropriate or welcome;
- publishing another person's private information without clear permission;
- sustained disruption, bad-faith argument, or retaliation after a report; and
- other behavior that a reasonable person would consider unsafe or
  unprofessional in this community.

Disagreement about code, evidence, design, or policy is allowed. Critique the
work and its effects, not the worth or identity of the person.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release channels, project events, and other spaces managed by Forecast Ledger
CLI. It also applies when someone officially represents the project, such as by
using an official account or email address or speaking as an appointed project
representative.

Private conduct outside those situations is not governed by this policy unless
it directly targets community participation or creates a credible safety risk
inside a project space.

## Report an incident privately

Do not open a public issue for a conduct incident. Email
[andrey@chaoscondensate.com](mailto:andrey@chaoscondensate.com) with the subject
`Forecast Ledger conduct report`. Andrey Korchak is the current Community
Manager and Community Moderator responsible for receiving and enforcing this
policy.

Include only what is safe for you to share:

- where and when the incident happened;
- what occurred, with links or screenshots if available;
- whether the behavior is continuing or anyone is in immediate danger;
- witnesses or earlier related incidents, if relevant; and
- how the moderator can contact you safely.

Do not include protected keys, credentials, unrevealed forecast content, or a
private ledger unless the incident cannot be understood without it. Redact
unrelated personal information.

If the report concerns the current moderator, say so in the subject. The
moderator must not decide that report and must arrange an uninvolved reviewer
acceptable to the reporter before the report details are shared further. The
project currently has one designated moderator, so this recusal step may delay
review; this limitation is recorded openly rather than offering a false
independent channel.

## What happens after a report

The person handling the report will:

1. acknowledge receipt through the reporter's safe contact method;
2. check for immediate safety, confidentiality, and conflict-of-interest needs;
3. explain who will review the report and when the next update is expected;
4. collect only the information needed to make a fair decision;
5. give the reported person a chance to respond when doing so is safe;
6. decide and document proportionate action; and
7. tell the reporter when review is complete, within privacy limits.

No fixed response time or outcome is guaranteed. Reports are handled as
promptly as maintainer capacity and safety allow. The reporter may ask for a
particular contact method, withdraw participation, or identify information
that must not be shared. Confidential details are limited to people needed for
review and are not published without permission, except where required by law
or needed to address a credible immediate threat.

Good-faith reports and participation in a review must not lead to retaliation.
Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves violate this policy;
unproven or mistaken reports do not.

## Enforcement

The Community Moderator may edit or remove content, pause an interaction,
issue a private correction or warning, restrict participation temporarily, or
remove access permanently. Decisions consider the behavior, its impact,
whether it is repeated, immediate safety, willingness to repair harm, and the
need to protect the community. Serious behavior may move directly to a strong
restriction.

Moderation decisions should state the relevant behavior and expected change.
Public explanations should identify the policy applied without exposing the
reporter or unnecessary private details. Project maintainers are subject to the
same policy and may lose their role or access when they do not follow or enforce
it fairly.
